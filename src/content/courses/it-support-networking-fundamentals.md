## Description

"IT Support and Networking Fundamentals" is your entry point into an IT career — no prior experience needed. You'll learn how computers and networks actually work, then practice the troubleshooting and support skills employers hire for.

Early modules build foundations in hardware, operating systems, and networking through hands-on setups. Then you'll master a repeatable troubleshooting method, system administration, and security fundamentals.

The course ends with cloud support skills and a helpdesk simulation capstone, where you clear a realistic ticket queue — direct proof you're ready for a support role.
