## Description

"Content Creation for Social Media" is your shortcut to consistent, high-quality posts that grow an audience. No expensive gear required — just your phone, a clear niche, and systems that make publishing easy.

You'll learn to find your niche, shoot clean video, and edit for retention with hooks and pacing that hold attention. Then you'll master short-form formats with templates you can reuse weekly.

The course ends with growth and monetization strategies plus a 30-day content sprint capstone, so you leave with finished posts and a calendar — not just ideas.
