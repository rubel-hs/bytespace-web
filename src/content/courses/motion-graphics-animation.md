## Description

"Motion Graphics and Animation Essentials" teaches you to make designs move with purpose. From core animation principles to polished exports, you'll build the skills behind ads, explainers, and social content that grabs attention.

You'll start with timing and easing, then develop an efficient After Effects workflow. Mid-course modules cover kinetic typography, animated infographics, and simple character animation.

The course wraps with sound design, platform-optimized exports, and a 30-second animated capstone piece — the perfect anchor for your motion showreel.
