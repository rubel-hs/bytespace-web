## Description

"Personal Finance and Investing Basics" gives you a calm, jargon-free path to financial control. No get-rich schemes — just budgeting, debt payoff, and investing fundamentals that work for real incomes.

You'll build a budget that actually sticks, attack debt with proven payoff methods, and start investing in index funds with confidence. Later modules cover emergency funds, insurance, and retirement accounts in plain language.

Your capstone is a one-page financial plan tying it all together — a document that guides your money decisions for years.
