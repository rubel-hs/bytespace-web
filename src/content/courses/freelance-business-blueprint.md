## Description

The "Freelance Business Blueprint" is a practical guide to turning skills into a sustainable solo business. No fluff — just positioning, pricing, pipelines, and operations you can implement immediately.

You'll start by picking a profitable niche and crafting an offer that sells. Then you'll learn value-based pricing, outreach systems, and proposals that close deals without awkward sales calls.

Later modules cover delivery, client experience, and money management, ending with a capstone where you publish your freelance offer page and launch your outreach plan.
