## Description

"UX Research and Usability Testing" teaches you to replace opinions with evidence. You'll learn to plan studies, talk to users, and translate findings into design decisions that measurably improve products.

The course starts with research planning and user interviews, then goes deep on moderated usability testing — the highest-leverage skill for any senior designer. You'll also cover surveys, metrics, and synthesis methods like affinity mapping and journey maps.

Your capstone is a complete research case study, from plan to recommendations, ready to strengthen your portfolio and your influence on product teams.
