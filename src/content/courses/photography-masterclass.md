## Description

"Photography Masterclass: Shoot Like a Pro" takes you from auto mode to intentional, gallery-worthy photography. Whether you shoot on a DSLR, mirrorless, or phone, you'll learn to see light and compose with purpose.

You'll start by mastering manual mode, then train your eye with light and composition lessons. Portrait modules teach you to direct real people with confidence, and Lightroom lessons build a fast, repeatable editing workflow.

Finally, you'll learn the client side — pricing, galleries, delivery — and finish with a themed photo series capstone worthy of exhibition or portfolio.
