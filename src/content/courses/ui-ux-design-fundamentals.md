## Description

Start your design journey with "UI/UX Design Fundamentals: From Wireframe to Prototype." This beginner-friendly course takes you from zero to a working prototype, teaching you how to think like a designer and build interfaces people love to use.

In the early modules, you'll learn what makes great UX by studying usability principles and real-world examples. You'll practice wireframing to structure ideas quickly before committing to visual details.

As you progress, you'll dive into Figma to build clickable prototypes, apply visual design basics, and run simple usability tests. By the end, you'll complete a full app redesign for your portfolio and know how to hand off your work to developers.
