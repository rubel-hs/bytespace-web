## Description

"Digital Marketing Mastery" gives you a complete, channel-by-channel playbook for growing brands online. Whether you're a freelancer, founder, or in-house marketer, you'll learn to combine organic and paid strategies into one coherent growth engine.

Early modules cover SEO foundations and content that converts, so you can earn compounding traffic. Then you'll move into paid ads, social growth systems, and email automation that turn attention into revenue.

Finally, you'll learn analytics and attribution to prove what's working — and package everything into a 90-day growth plan you can reuse for any client or business.
