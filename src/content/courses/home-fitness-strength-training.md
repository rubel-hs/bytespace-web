## Description

"Home Fitness and Strength Training" proves you don't need a gym to get strong and energetic. Coach-led lessons take you from safe movement basics to a complete 8-week training program.

You'll master form first, then follow progressive bodyweight and dumbbell workouts paired with sustainable cardio. Nutrition lessons keep fueling simple — protein, hydration, and planning without restrictive diets.

Recovery, mobility, and habit systems keep you consistent, and your capstone is a personalized 8-week plan you'll actually start during the course.
