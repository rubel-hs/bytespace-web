## Description

In "Brand Identity Design: Logos to Design Systems," you'll learn to build brands that stick — from strategic foundations to polished deliverables. This course bridges the gap between pretty logos and complete, client-ready identities.

You'll begin with brand strategy, defining positioning and personality that guide every design decision. Then you'll work through a professional logo process, from sketching to vector refinement.

Later modules teach you to build design systems, write usable brand guidelines, and present identities across mockups. Your capstone is a full brand case study ready for your portfolio or next client pitch.
